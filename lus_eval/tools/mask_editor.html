Draw the liver mask

  • Each image starts from SAM's mask. Fix it, or press Clear and draw from scratch.
  • Brush paints liver, Eraser removes it. Hold Shift to erase with the brush.
  • Loop add / Loop remove: draw around a region to fill it in or cut it out.
  • Mark the visible liver surface only: leave out the probe, tools, ligament and fat on it.
  • Zoom with the scroll wheel; pan by holding Space and dragging, or right-drag. F fits the image.
  • When an image looks right, press Done (Enter). At the end, Download and tell Claude.
