Mark the liver

  1. Box: drag a box tightly around the visible liver. The tool then switches to Liver.
  2. Liver: click for a point, or drag to scribble, on the liver. If a tool or the probe splits the liver, mark each part.
  3. Not liver: click or scribble on anything inside the box that is not liver: the ultrasound probe, tools, fat, other organs.
  4. Corrections, only after looking at the result: Add area / Remove area: draw a loop around a region; it is put into / taken out of the final mask as drawn, without SAM.
  5. Not sure what is liver? Press K to show Koo et al.'s expert lines for this image: silhouette, ridge (the liver's sharp lower edge), ligament. They only mark liver edges, not the whole liver.
  6. When all 46 are done, press Download and tell Claude.
